Privacy and Safety Briefing

AI Prompt Builder is a client-side tool. Nothing you type is stored or sent to an external server.

Staying Safe with AI: What You Need to Know

1. Is it safe to type my notes into this website?

Yes, completely. Everything you type stays right here on your own screen. Nothing you write is sent to an outside company, saved on the internet, or seen by anyone else. When you click Copy, nothing leaves your browser until you paste it somewhere yourself.

2. What happens when I paste my prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot?

This website is a helper tool to organize your thoughts. The AI chat tool you paste into is a separate online service. The moment you paste your prompt into it, that outside company receives your words. Some free tools save your input to help train their systems, so be mindful of what you include.

3. The 3 Simple Rules to Keep Your Information Safe

  • Rule 1: The "Noticeboard Rule" — Never paste anything into an AI tool that you wouldn't feel comfortable pinning to a public noticeboard (no passwords, bank details, home addresses, or private medical notes).
  • Rule 2: Swap Real Names for "Fake Names" — Replace real people and companies with generic labels.
  • Rule 3: Use Your Official Work or School Login — If your employer or school provides an official AI account, use that instead of your personal email.
