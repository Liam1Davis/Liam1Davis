Staying Safe with AI: What You Need to Know

1. Is it safe to type here?

Yes. Everything you type stays in your own browser — nothing is sent anywhere until you copy it out yourself.

2. What happens when you paste the compiled prompt elsewhere?

The moment you paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or any other AI tool, that outside company receives it. Some free tools save your input to help train their systems, so be mindful of what you include in Context or Primary Directive.

3. The 3 Simple Rules to Keep Your Information Safe

  • Rule 1: The "Noticeboard Rule" — Never include anything you wouldn't pin to a public noticeboard (no passwords, bank details, home addresses, or private medical notes).
  • Rule 2: Swap Real Names for "Fake Names" — Replace real people and companies with generic labels.
  • Rule 3: Use Your Official Work or School Login — If your employer or school provides an official AI account, use that instead of your personal email.
