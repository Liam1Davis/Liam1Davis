🔒 Privacy & Safety Briefing

Staying Safe with AI: What You Need to Know

1. Is it safe to type my notes into this website?

Yes, completely. Everything you type stays right here in your own browser — nothing is sent to an outside company or saved on the internet.

2. What happens when I paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot?

This website is just a helper tool to organize your thoughts. The AI chat tool is a separate online service — the moment you paste your prompt there, that outside company receives your words. Some free tools save your questions to help train their systems, so always be mindful of what you paste.

3. The 3 Simple Rules to Keep Your Information Safe

  • Rule 1: The "Noticeboard Rule" — Never paste anything you wouldn't feel comfortable pinning to a public noticeboard (no passwords, bank details, home addresses, or private medical notes).
  • Rule 2: Swap Real Names for "Fake Names" — Replace real people and companies with generic labels.
  • Rule 3: Use Your Official Work or School Login — If your employer or school provides an official AI account, use that instead of your personal email.
