Staying Safe with AI: What You Need to Know

1. Is it safe to type my notes into this website?

Yes, completely. Think of this website like a digital notepad on your desk:

  • Everything you type stays right here on your own computer screen.
  • Nothing you write is sent to an outside company, saved on the internet, or seen by anyone else.
  • When you click "Copy", nothing leaves your browser until you paste it somewhere yourself.

2. What happens when I paste my prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot?

This is the most important thing to understand:

  • This website is just a helper tool to organize your thoughts.
  • The AI chat tool (like ChatGPT or Copilot) is a separate online service that answers your questions.

The moment you copy your prompt and paste it into an AI chat box, that outside company receives your words. Some free tools save your questions to help train their systems, so you should always be mindful of what you paste.

3. The 3 Simple Rules to Keep Your Information Safe

  • Rule 1: The "Noticeboard Rule" — Never paste anything into an AI tool that you wouldn't feel comfortable pinning to a public noticeboard at work or school (no passwords, bank details, home addresses, or private medical notes).
  • Rule 2: Swap Real Names for "Fake Names" — Replace real people and companies with generic labels (e.g. write "Help me reply to an email from a client at a bank" instead of using real names).
  • Rule 3: Use Your Official Work or School Login — If your employer or school provides an official AI account (like Microsoft Copilot), always use that account instead of your personal email to keep your data protected under organizational agreements.
